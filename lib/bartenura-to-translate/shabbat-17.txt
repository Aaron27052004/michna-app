[[COMMENT shabbat|17|3|1]]
a reed that is made to check the olives that are in the pit or vat where olives are packed until they form a viscid mass, if their oil was gathered in them and that they were worthy of the olive press.
[[/COMMENT]]

[[COMMENT shabbat|17|3|2]]
a sort of stopper that is in the reed is susceptible for ritual impurity because it is stopped up at the knot, it is left over in it from the oil that flows from the olives and he checks it if it arrived to stopping and it is a receptacle for him.
[[/COMMENT]]

[[COMMENT shabbat|17|3|3]]
if it has no knot, even though it is hollowed, he did not hollow it out to receive anything. Therefore, he has a flat wooden implement (see Talmud Shabbat 123b) and is not susceptible for ritual impurity.
[[/COMMENT]]

[[COMMENT shabbat|17|3|4]]
for it is an implement and it is used to turn over the olives.
[[/COMMENT]]

[[COMMENT shabbat|17|4|1]]
- a large saw that we saw boards.
[[/COMMENT]]

[[COMMENT shabbat|17|4|2]]
a large utensil made like a knife with which they make a incision of a turn of the plough/furrow’s length and they are strict with him and designated for them a place that is not worthy for any other labor.
[[/COMMENT]]

[[COMMENT shabbat|17|4|3]]
this is how it should be understood: All the utensils whose labor is for permitted things such as plates and cups which are removed [on the Sabbath] for the necessity of its own body as well as for the necessity of the place of the utensil, as well as that which is not necessary, even if he doesn’t need the implement nor the place of the utensil, other than to carry it on account of the shade or that thieves should not steal it, it is permitted with a utensil that whose labor is something permitted. But a utensil that is used for some kind of prohibited labor, such as mortars and millstone and similar things for its own necessity and for the needs of its place, it is permitted, but from the sun to the shade or because of thieves, it is prohibited.
[[/COMMENT]]

[[COMMENT shabbat|17|4|4]]
It is explained in the Talmud (Talmud Shabbat 124b) for the needs of its unique use alone but not for the needs of something else, even for its own needs, such as a knife to cut with alone, it is permitted, and not to support with it the bowl. But the Halakha is not according to Rabbi Nehemiah.
[[/COMMENT]]

[[COMMENT shabbat|17|5|1]]
some kind of work that will be and even if it is not the kind of the first work.
[[/COMMENT]]

[[COMMENT shabbat|17|5|2]]
thick [porridge] that is similar to started dough that is mixed in water and they do not dispute other than if they were broken on Shabbat, but if they were broken on the Eve of the Sabbath, everyone agrees that they are removed, even if they don’t do a kind of the first kind of work. But the Halakha is not according to Rabbi Yehuda.
[[/COMMENT]]

[[COMMENT shabbat|17|6|4]]

[[/COMMENT]]

[[COMMENT shabbat|17|7|1]]
such as a tablet or a curtain or other things that they close a window with.
[[/COMMENT]]

[[COMMENT shabbat|17|7|2]]
that is not dragged on the ground.
[[/COMMENT]]

[[COMMENT shabbat|17|7|3]]
for if it was dragged on the ground when it is loosened from the ground to close it, it appears like adding to the building, and Rabbi Eliezer holds that we don’t add to a temporary tent on Shabbat.
[[/COMMENT]]

[[COMMENT shabbat|17|7|4]]
whether it is tied or not tied, we shutter it up with it, since it was prepared/ready from the day before, for this the Rabbis hold that we add on to a temporary tent on the Sabbath, and the Halakha is according to the Sages.
[[/COMMENT]]

[[COMMENT shabbat|17|8|1]]
In the Talmud (Tractate Shabbat 126b), that regarding a lid of vessels, everyone agrees that it is permitted, even though it lacks a handle, and with ground openings, such as the covering for a pit and a cistern no one disputes that it is forbidden if it lacks a handle, but they argue with a handle of a utensil attached to the ground; one opinion holds that it is like the ground and the other opinion holds that is not like the ground; but the Halakha is according to the Sages that what is attached to the ground is like the ground.
[[/COMMENT]]
