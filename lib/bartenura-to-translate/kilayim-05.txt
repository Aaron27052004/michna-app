And this is what obliged the Tanna to state that this sanctifies forty-five vines, and even after the earliest root is sixteen cubits in every direction, he had to shorten it and state this is sanctifies sixteen cubits in every direction , etc. And much that we have written has already been solved, for it is impossible to state that something is five cubits separated by five cubits in four directions that he sanctifies sixteen cubits in every direction because he sanctifies twenty-cubits in every direction, and therefore, he states that he sanctifies forty-five vines, and that is impossible other than after we add to the circle as we have explained clearly.
