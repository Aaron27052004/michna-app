[[COMMENT negaim|9|1|1]]
if in their place (i.e., the boil or the burning) was born/originated the bright white spot on the skin, we shut him up/quarantine/isolate him and at the end of one week if there is white hair or spreading, he (i.e., the Kohen) declares him to be a certified leper, and if not, he releases him. But with quick flesh he is not defiled, for quick flesh requires live flesh, and this is not live flesh since in its place is boils and burning.
[[/COMMENT]]

[[COMMENT negaim|9|1|2]]
(a pressed hard mass, peat, turf)- refuse of olives, and it is hot on account of itself. And similarly, the hot springs of Tiberias through the heat of their source they are hot and not in consequence of the fire.
[[/COMMENT]]

[[COMMENT negaim|9|1|3]]
to include the lead directly from the mine (hot – see Tractate Hullin 8a).
[[/COMMENT]]

[[COMMENT negaim|9|1|4]]
the ashes of a portable stove on feet (with caves for two pots). And it is hot on account of the fire.
[[/COMMENT]]

[[COMMENT negaim|9|1|5]]
and it is hot from the effect of/in consequence of the fire. – to include the heat of the fire.
[[/COMMENT]]

[[COMMENT negaim|9|2|1]]
one-half a split [Cilician] bean of boils with one-half a split [Cilician] bean of burning do not combine [to make] a complete split [Cilician] bean.
[[/COMMENT]]

[[COMMENT negaim|9|2|2]]
that if a boil and a burning are one next to the other, and in one of them is a bright white spot on the skin the size of a split [Cilician] bean, and he was shut up/isolated/quarantined, and at the end of the week, it spread to its neighbor or on the skin of the flesh, we don’t declare him a certified leper. And similarly, if the bright white spot was on the skin of the flesh and spread to the boil or to the burn, it is not considered spreading.
[[/COMMENT]]

[[COMMENT negaim|9|2|3]]
that they didn’t heal properly and the skin did not have a scab on it, and it still produces secretion, we don’t defile it through plagues, as it is written (Leviticus 13:18): “When an inflammation (i.e., boil) appears on the skin of one’s body and it heals.,” but with a burning, it is written (Leviticus 13:24): " "/ “and the patch from the burn is a dis-coloration, [either white streaked with rea, or white.]”
[[/COMMENT]]

[[COMMENT negaim|9|2|4]]
that it is recognized that there was a boil there or a burn and it is not equivalent to the rest of the skin of the flesh, nevertheless it is judged like the skin of the flesh, for after it became a strong scab.
[[/COMMENT]]
