[[COMMENT meilah|4|3|6]]

[[COMMENT meilah|4|3|7]]
as, for example, carrion and reptile as their uncleanness is similar, and both of them there are ritually unclean until evening, but not their requisite measure, [since] for carrion, the measure of its uncleanness is an olives bulk, whereas for reptiles, its measure is a lentil’s bulk.
[[/COMMENT]]

[[COMMENT meilah|4|3|8]]

[[/COMMENT]]

[[COMMENT meilah|4|3|9]]

But with its requisite measure also, they are not equal/equivalent, for the measure of defilement with the dead is an olive’s bulk, and the measure of a reptile is as a lentil’s bulk.
[[/COMMENT]]

[[COMMENT meilah|4|4|2]]

And similar, half of an olive’s bulk of a dead corpse does not combine to a half of an olive’s bulk of carrion to become impure, even for the impurity until evening.

[[/COMMENT]]
