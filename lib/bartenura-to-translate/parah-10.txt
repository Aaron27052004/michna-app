[[COMMENT parah|10|2|1]]
The upper part of a person with gonorrhea is called a /Maddaf for if there were on top of a person with gonorrhea tent-covers one on top of another, all of them are impure through indirect contact for conferring ritual impurity, even the uppermost one that the person with gonorrhea did not have contact with. Just as [the case of] ten mattresses/beds one on top of the other and a person with gonorrhea lay down on the uppermost one, all of them are ritually impure according to the Torah because of lying, and even though he didn’t come in contact/touch the lowest ones, so also the upper tent-covers that are upon the person with gonorrhea, all of them are impure according to the Rabbis because of indirect contact/ and even though he did not come in contact with them. But there is a distinction between lying to indirect contact by shaking/, that the person [who defiles] by lying becomes a primary source of ritual uncleanness/ to defile people and vessels, but the /indirect contact through shaking is not other than a secondary source of impurity/ to defile food and liquids but because of the stringency of the purification/sin-offering, people and vessels that came in contact in an indirect manner through shaking/ are impure for a purification/sin-offering (see also Tractate Eduyot, Chapter 6, Mishnah 2 and Tractate Zavim, Chapter 4, Mishnah 6).
[[/COMMENT]]
